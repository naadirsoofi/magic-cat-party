# Magic Cat Party Assets

This folder contains audio and image assets for the Magic Cat Party game.

## Audio Files Needed:

For the best experience, add these audio files to the assets folder:

### Required Audio Files:
- `background-music.mp3` - Soft, playful background music loop
- `meow.mp3` - Cat meow sound effect
- `boing.mp3` - Bouncy sound effect
- `cheer.mp3` - Celebration/cheer sound
- `pop.mp3` - Pop sound effect
- `whoosh.mp3` - Whoosh sound for rocket
- `magic.mp3` - Magical sound effect

### Where to Find Free Audio:
1. **Freesound.org** - Free sound effects with attribution
2. **YouTube Audio Library** - Free music and sound effects
3. **Incompetech.com** - Free music by Kevin MacLeod
4. **Pixabay Audio** - Free audio clips
5. **Free Music Archive** - Creative Commons music

### Audio Requirements:
- Format: MP3 (most compatible)
- Length: Sound effects 1-3 seconds, background music can loop
- Volume: Keep consistent levels, not too loud for children

### Cat GIFs:
The game uses online cat GIFs from Giphy. No local images needed, but you can replace the URLs in `script.js` if you prefer local files.

### Example Sound Sources:
- Meow: Search "cat meow" on freesound.org
- Boing: Search "bounce" or "spring" sounds
- Cheer: Search "kids cheer" or "yay"
- Pop: Search "bubble pop" or "balloon pop"
- Whoosh: Search "whoosh" or "swoosh"
- Magic: Search "magic spell" or "chime"
- Background: Search "children's music" or "happy loop"

**Note:** The game will work without audio files - it just won't play sounds. All visual effects will still work perfectly!
